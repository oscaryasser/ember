Ember: Recomp Coach — help & contact

Ember is an offline body-recomposition coach for iPhone: log your weight, food and macros, runs, and Push / Pull / Legs lifts, and get coaching on pace, progression, and recovery. Everything runs on your device — no account needed.

Need help or have a question?
Email oscaryasser@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within 1–2 days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version so we can help faster.

Getting started

On the Today tab, type your weight and set a target, tap what you trained (Run / Push / Pull / Legs), and log food to fill the calorie ring. The Coach tab gives you a weekly verdict; the Plan tab explains the run, pull-up, and lifting programs; Progress shows your trends and photos; Goals lets you change every number the coach judges you against and back up your data.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? Is it private?

All of it stays on your device — there are no accounts, servers, ads, or tracking. See our Privacy Policy. You can export a full JSON backup any time from the Goals tab.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?

Goals tab → Export backup (JSON) to save a file (or Copy to clipboard). On the new device, Goals tab → Import backup file or Paste backup JSON. Progress photos are stored separately on-device; save the ones you want to keep from the Progress tab.

Apple Health isn’t connecting — what do I do?

Apple Health is optional; Ember works fully with manual entry, which always takes priority. If connecting doesn’t work: open Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Ember and enable the categories, then reopen Ember and tap Sync now. If it still doesn’t import, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version and we’ll help.

An entry didn’t save.

Ember saves automatically to two on-device stores. If you ever see a “save failed” notice, export a backup from Goals immediately and email us. Force-quitting the app right after typing on older iOS versions is the usual cause — give it a second before switching away.

How do the training programs and progression work?

The Plan tab has a full write-up for each: the 10-week run plan, the grease-the-groove pull-up program, and the Push/Pull/Legs lifting guide (double progression, reps-in-reserve, when to deload, and how to avoid injury). In Gym Mode each lift tells you its next move from your own history.

Is Ember a substitute for a doctor or trainer?

No. Ember offers general fitness guidance only. Stop and consult a professional for sharp or persistent joint pain, or before starting a new program if you have a medical condition.
