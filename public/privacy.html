Ember. Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Ember collects nothing. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything you log lives entirely on your device.

What Ember stores, and where

Your weigh-ins, workouts, food, measurements, and progress photos are saved only in your device's local app storage (browser localStorage + IndexedDB on the web, and the app's local WebView storage on iPhone). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. You can export a JSON backup at any time from the Goals tab — that file is created on-device and only goes wherever you choose to save it.

Apple Health (optional)

If you connect Apple Health, Ember requests read-only access to your body mass, steps, sleep, active energy, and workouts, and uses them only to fill in your daily log on the device. Ember never writes anything back to Health, never shares Health data with third parties, never uses it for advertising or analytics, and never transmits it off your device. You can disconnect at any time in the Goals tab, or revoke access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Ember.

No data sharing or selling

Because nothing you enter ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or lose. We have no access to your data.

Children

Ember is a general fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13.
