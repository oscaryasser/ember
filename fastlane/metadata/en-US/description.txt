Ember turns your daily runs, lifts, food, and weigh-ins into one clear verdict: are you actually losing fat while keeping muscle?

WHAT EMBER DOES
• Today — log weight, protein, calories in/out, steps, and sleep in seconds. A live ring shows exactly where you stand.
• Gym Mode — run your Push / Pull / Legs sessions with a rest timer, warm-up ramps, plate math, and previous-vs-today numbers for real progressive overload.
• Coach — a plain-English weekly verdict (eat more, push harder, or hold the line) based on your own trend, not generic rules.
• Progress — weight trend, strength and pull-up charts, body measurements, and progress photos.
• Plan — a grease-the-groove pull-up program and a simple tap-to-plan weekly schedule.

APPLE HEALTH (OPTIONAL)
Connect Apple Health and Ember auto-fills your weight, steps, sleep, active energy, and workouts. It only reads — nothing is ever written back — and anything you enter by hand always wins.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Everything lives on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing you log ever leaves your phone. Export a JSON backup whenever you want.

Ember is for people who lift and run and want to stop guessing.
